Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

This policy explains how DBO Properties Limited handles personal information when you visit our website or contact us about our properties and services.

Information you provide

When you contact us, request property information, or arrange a viewing, you may provide information such as your name, phone number, email address, enquiry details, and property preferences. Please do not send sensitive personal or financial information unless it is necessary for a specific transaction and we have explained why it is needed.

How we use information

We use the information you provide to respond to enquiries, share requested property details, arrange viewings, provide our services, communicate about a transaction, and maintain the security and operation of our website. We may also use it to meet applicable legal obligations and protect our rights.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information with property owners, professional advisers, or service providers where reasonably necessary to respond to your request, provide a service, operate the website, or comply with law. Those parties should only use the information for the relevant purpose and as permitted by applicable requirements.

External links and communications

Our website may link to services operated by third parties, including WhatsApp, social networks, maps, and video platforms. If you follow an external link or contact us through one of those services, that service's own privacy policy and terms apply to its handling of your information.

Cookies and technical information

Your browser or hosting provider may process basic technical information when you visit a website, such as connection and device information, to deliver and secure the site. This website may use essential browser storage or cookies where needed for site functions. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some functionality.

Retention and security

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected, including responding to your enquiry, providing services, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards intended to protect information, but no website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request or raise a privacy concern, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Children's privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults seeking property information and services. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through this website.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when it was most recently revised. Continued use of the website after a change means the updated policy is available for your review.
